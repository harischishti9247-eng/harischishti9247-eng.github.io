About Muhammad Haris Chishti

Muhammad Haris Chishti is a Pakistani Naat Khuwan, Qawwal, Musician and Naat Composer from Akbarpura, Nowshera, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan.

He is passionate about Islamic devotional music and spiritual poetry. His artistic work includes Naat Sharif, Hamd-e-Bari Ta'ala, Manqabat-e-Auliya, Naatia Qawwali and Sufi Kalam.

He performs in Urdu, Pashto, Punjabi, Arabic and Persian, bringing the beauty of devotional poetry to audiences around the world.

Through his official YouTube channel and digital music platforms, Muhammad Haris Chishti shares his devotional performances and musical creations with listeners worldwide.

Profession: Naat Khuwan, Qawwal, Musician and Naat Composer.

Hometown: Akbarpura, Nowshera, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan.

Languages: Urdu, Pashto, Punjabi, Arabic and Persian.

Music Categories: Hamd, Naat, Manqabat, Naatia Qawwali and Sufi Kalam.
