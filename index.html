About Muhammad Haris Chishti

Muhammad Haris Chishti is a Naat Khuwan, Qawwal, musician and Naat composer from Akbarpura, Nowshera, Pakistan. His work includes Hamd-e-Pak, Naat Sharif, Manqabat-e-Auliya and Naatia Qawwali.

He performs in Urdu, Pashto, Punjabi, Arabic and Persian, sharing devotional kalam with audiences around the world.
